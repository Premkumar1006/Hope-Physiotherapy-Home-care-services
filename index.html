FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide physiotherapy at home?

Home-visit enquiries are welcome for selected areas in Hyderabad and Secunderabad. Share your locality so availability can be checked.

Which conditions can I enquire about?

You can enquire about neuro rehabilitation, orthopaedic conditions, back and neck pain, elderly mobility and post-surgical rehabilitation. Suitability is determined after assessment.

How do I enquire about an appointment?

Call or WhatsApp +91 88979 78832 with your name, locality and the type of support required.

No. This website collects enquiries only. It does not diagnose conditions or replace an in-person clinical assessment or urgent medical care.
